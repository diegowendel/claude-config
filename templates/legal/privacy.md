## When you contact us

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send: usually your name, email address and message. Contact links open your own email app, so your message reaches us as an ordinary email.

- **Purpose:** to answer your message and to fix problems you report.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and improving the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** we keep correspondence for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete it.
- **Processors:** messages are handled by our email provider, which may process data outside the European Economic Area under appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

We never sell your information or use it for marketing.

## Our website

The website is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. When you visit it, Google processes your IP address and basic request details (such as the page requested and your browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and keeps them in server logs for a limited period. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

## Information the app stores provide

Apple and Google may share anonymous crash reports and aggregated store statistics (such as download counts) with developers, depending on your device settings. These are governed by their own privacy policies and can't be used to identify you.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.
